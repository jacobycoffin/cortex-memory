# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Welcome questions from people new to Hermes, memory research, or Python. Critique claims and implementations without attacking people. Do not post private conversations, memories, credentials, or identifying data from another person's agent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate exposure of private data are not acceptable. Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect the community.
