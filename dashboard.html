Sources in docs/BRAIN_FOUNDATIONS.md
P01 · ATTENTION

Recall only when useful

A fast gate can abstain on greetings and self-contained work, or open a larger budget for personal, temporal, and multi-hop tasks.

Working-memory limits · Baddeley and Cowan
P02 · ACTIVATION

Use strengthens access

Recency, frequency, confirmed usefulness, and memory type influence activation. Retrieval alone does not count as success.

Rational analysis of memory · Anderson and Schooler
P03 · ASSOCIATION

Related ideas can spread

Lexical search, transparent semantic features, and bounded graph activation find neighbors without an extra model call.

Spreading activation · Collins and Loftus
P04 · RECONSOLIDATION

Correction keeps history

New evidence versions a belief instead of silently overwriting it. Dependents become reviewable when their evidence changes.

Reconsolidation research · Nader and Hardt
P05 · CONSOLIDATION

Repetition becomes structure

Near-duplicates can fold behind a canonical memory, while repeated tool steps become a procedure. Every fold is reversible.

Complementary learning systems · McClelland et al.
P06 · ADAPTIVE FORGETTING

Stale is cooled, not erased

Low-value memories move through active, cold, and archived states. Regret detection can surface or restore an over-pruned item.

Adaptive forgetting and systems consolidation
